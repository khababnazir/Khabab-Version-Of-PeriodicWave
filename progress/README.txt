PROGRESS LOG
============

One plain-text file per working day, named YYYY-MM-DD.txt.

Written in ordinary prose, first person, the way you would tell a colleague
what happened. Not bullet-point telegraphese. Someone reading it six months
from now -- including you -- should be able to follow what was done and why.

THE RULE
--------
An entry is written only after Khabab has confirmed he understood what was
done that day. Not "Claude explained it" -- confirmed, by saying it back or
answering a question about it. If the day ended with something still unclear,
that goes in the entry as an open question and the entry says so plainly.
The log is a record of understanding, not a record of activity.

WHAT GOES WHERE
---------------
progress/YYYY-MM-DD.txt   chronological diary: what happened today, by whom
notes/stageNN.md          the physics and the ML: derivations, checkpoint
                          answers, things that confused you and why they
                          stopped confusing you

The progress log is the narrative. The notes are the understanding. Keep them
separate -- they get read for different reasons.
