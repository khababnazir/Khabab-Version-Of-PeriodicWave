WHAT WE SET OUT TO DO
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build the machinery that turns a wave function into an energy. The energy of
a trial state is E = <Psi|H|Psi> / <Psi|Psi>, a 2N-dimensional integral we
cannot do directly. Variational Monte Carlo replaces it with an average: draw
configurations R from |Psi(R)|^2, evaluate the local energy
E_loc(R) = (H Psi)(R) / Psi(R) at each one, and average (paper Sec. IV A,
Eqs. 24-28). Two pieces: a sampler, and the local energy.

WHAT CLAUDE DID

1. learn/stage04_mcmc/metropolis.py -- THE SAMPLER

   Metropolis-Hastings, following the authors' mcmc.py:
     - propose: move every electron of every walker by a Gaussian step
     - accept if  log(uniform) < log|Psi'|^2 - log|Psi|^2,  else stay put
   Only the RATIO |Psi'|^2 / |Psi|^2 is ever needed, so the normalisation of
   Psi -- the integral we cannot do -- cancels and is never computed. With a
   symmetric proposal this acceptance rule makes |Psi|^2 the distribution the
   walkers settle into (detailed balance).

   Step width follows the authors' rule from train.py: multiply by 1.1 if
   more than 55% of moves are accepted, by 0.9 if fewer than 50%. The authors
   adjust every 100 training iterations of 30 steps each; we adjust every 10
   steps so a short burn-in can tune it. Important, from the review: the
   width is tuned during burn-in ONLY, then frozen while measuring. The
   guarantee that walkers follow |Psi|^2 belongs to a fixed rule; a rule that
   keeps changing itself from the walkers' own history is not covered.

2. learn/stage04_mcmc/local_energy.py -- THE LOCAL ENERGY

   Writing Psi = exp(u + i*phi), with u = log|Psi| and phi the phase:
       (Lap Psi)/Psi = Lap u + |grad u|^2 - |grad phi|^2
                       + i (Lap phi + 2 grad u . grad phi)
   so the kinetic energy is built entirely from derivatives of log Psi --
   which Stage 3 already computes. We keep the imaginary part. The authors'
   fast path (folx) drops it; whether that matters is a Stage 6 question.

   Potential: the moire potential only. Coulomb repulsion needs Ewald
   summation under periodic boundary conditions, which is Stage 7, so every
   Stage 4 number is for NON-interacting electrons and is not comparable to
   anything in the paper yet.

   One convention trap found and documented: the paper writes the moire
   vectors g_j at 0/120/240 degrees; the authors' code uses the same star
   rotated by 90 degrees (90/330/210). With the code's lattice orientation
   only the code's vectors make the potential periodic. We use the code's,
   and a test checks our potential equals the authors' function to 1e-12.

3. learn/stage04_mcmc/estimate_energy.py -- PUTTING IT TOGETHER

   Run A, a Slater determinant of plane waves with no potential, is an exact
   eigenstate of the kinetic energy: exact energy 14.621636, and every sampled
   configuration gave exactly that (largest deviation 1.2e-13, variance
   3.6e-29). That is the zero-variance property of Eq. 28. It checks the
   kinetic energy. It does NOT check the sampler -- an eigenstate has the same
   E_loc everywhere, so even a stuck sampler would pass (a review correction).

   Run B, an untrained SlaterNet (6 electrons, 9 sites) in the moire
   potential: E = +46.199 +- 0.342 in code units (+155.95 +- 1.16 meV),
   variance of E_loc 782.8, mean imaginary part -0.023 +- 0.096 (zero within
   the error, as it must be). High, because the network is random; training
   in Stage 6 is what lowers it.

   The error bar treats the measurement rounds as independent. They are not
   quite -- each continues the same chains -- so it is approximate; a proper
   blocking analysis comes later (pyblock is already installed).

4. learn/stage04_mcmc/test_stage04.py -- FOUR CHECKS, absltest style

   - plane-wave determinant gives the exact kinetic energy at random points
   - our moire potential equals the authors' make_triangular_potential
   - the potential has the period of the moire lattice
   - Metropolis sampling a Gaussian |Psi|^2 reproduces mean 0, variance
     sigma^2 (burn in with tuning, then sample with the width frozen)

   Each test was shown to fail when the code is deliberately broken: deleting
   the -|grad phi|^2 term, swapping in the paper's moire vectors, and making
   the sampler accept every move were each caught.

5. FIXES FROM THE REVIEW (all four points were valid)

   - freeze the step width while measuring (point 1 above)
   - report the variance of the FULL complex E_loc, mean |E_loc - mean|^2.
     This is the quantity equal to <H^2> - <H>^2, since
     <H^2> = integral |H Psi|^2 = average of |E_loc|^2. The real part alone
     came out at 751.8, the full value 782.8.
   - describe the error bar as approximate rather than "slightly low"
   - run A does not validate the sampler (point 3 above)
